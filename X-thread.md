"Ndimbal" means *mutual aid* in Wolof 🇸🇳.

It reinvents the West African tontine on-chain: you save together, a few members win the pooled yield each round — and you can quietly give part of a win back to your community.

No names. No pressure.

🎟️ ticket = balance × FHE.randEuint16()
